How Adaptive Damping Works:

Stable patients operate at conservative thresholds (70–75%) to prevent non-actionable notification spam. When a patient's vitals demonstrate erratic volatility (e.g. accelerating tachycardia paired with MAP degradation), the controller automatically tightens the threshold down to 60%, providing safety margin before acute shock onset.

FDA CDS Alignment (SaMD Considerations):

Unlike black-box models, the Alert Budget Controller's boundary is deterministic and mathematically auditable. The bedside clinician can inspect the exact active threshold and variance ratio at any transaction timestamp.
